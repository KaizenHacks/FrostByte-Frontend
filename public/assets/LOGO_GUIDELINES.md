# KaizenHacks Logo Guidelines

## 1. Purpose

The KaizenHacks logo is the primary visual identifier of the organization.

These guidelines define how the approved KaizenHacks logo variants should be selected, positioned, scaled, colored, and reproduced.

The logo should remain consistent across digital, print, event, promotional, and organizational materials.

---

# 2. Logo System

KaizenHacks maintains several logo configurations for different applications.

### Primary

The **Primary Logo** is the default KaizenHacks logo.

Use it whenever there is sufficient space and no specific layout requirement calls for another version.

**Preferred for:**

- Brand presentations
- Official documents
- Main promotional materials
- Website branding
- General organizational communication
- Large-format applications

---

### Horizontal

The **Horizontal Logo** is designed for wide layouts.

Use it when vertical space is limited or when the surrounding layout naturally favors a horizontal composition.

**Preferred for:**

- Website headers
- Navigation areas
- Banners
- Email signatures
- Wide presentations
- Horizontal promotional layouts

---

### Stacked

The **Stacked Logo** is designed for more compact or vertically oriented compositions.

**Preferred for:**

- Posters
- Portrait layouts
- Social media graphics
- Square compositions
- Applications where the horizontal logo becomes too small

---

### Iconic

The **Iconic Logo** uses the recognizable symbol/mark without requiring the full wordmark.

Use it only where the KaizenHacks identity is already established or where space is highly limited.

**Preferred for:**

- Profile images
- App or digital icons
- Small branded spaces
- Social avatars
- Secondary brand placements

The iconic mark should not normally replace the full logo when KaizenHacks is being introduced to a new audience.

---

### Typographic

The **Typographic Logo** is the word-based logo configuration.

Use it when a typography-focused presentation is appropriate and the full visual mark is unnecessary.

It should remain consistent with the approved KaizenHacks typography system.

---

### Favicon

The **Favicon** is specifically intended for browser and small digital interface contexts.

**Preferred for:**

- Website favicon
- Browser tabs
- Web application icons
- Small digital identification

Do not use the favicon as a general-purpose replacement for the primary logo.

---

### Monochromatic

The **Monochromatic Logos** are single-color versions intended for situations where full-color reproduction is not appropriate.

Available versions include:

- Black
- White

**Preferred for:**

- Single-color printing
- Stamps
- Documents
- High-contrast applications
- Photography
- Dark or light surfaces where the full-color logo does not reproduce correctly

---

### Transparent Background

Transparent-background versions are provided for situations where the logo must sit directly over another surface.

The transparency does not represent a separate logo identity.

It is simply a file-format/application variation.

---

# 3. Logo Selection Hierarchy

When choosing a logo, use this order of preference:

1. **Primary Logo**
2. **Horizontal Logo**
3. **Stacked Logo**
4. **Iconic Logo**
5. **Typographic Logo**
6. **Monochromatic version when required**
7. **Favicon for favicon/small browser contexts only**

The correct logo is determined by the layout and application, not personal preference.

---

# 4. Logo Clear Space

Clear space is the minimum amount of empty space that should surround the logo.

No text, imagery, borders, icons, or other graphic elements should intrude into the logo's clear-space area.

The exact clear-space measurement should be defined from the final approved logo artwork.

Until a formal measurement is established, use a visually generous margin around the logo rather than placing it tightly against other elements.

### Never

- Place text directly against the logo
- Touch the logo with another graphic
- Allow photographs to visually interfere with the mark
- Treat the logo as a decorative element that can be crowded

The logo must always have room to breathe.

---

# 5. Minimum Size

The logo must remain large enough for its details and lettering to remain clearly recognizable.

Minimum sizes should be established separately for:

- Digital use
- Print use
- Large-format applications

Do not use a full logo at a size where the wordmark becomes difficult to read.

When the available space becomes too small, use the **Iconic Logo** or an appropriate compact version instead.

---

# 6. Backgrounds

The logo must maintain sufficient contrast against its background.

### On Light Backgrounds

Preferred:

- Standard full-color logo
- Dark/monochromatic version when appropriate

### On Dark Backgrounds

Preferred:

- Light logo variation
- White monochromatic version

### On Photography

The logo should only be placed over photography when there is sufficient contrast.

If necessary:

- Use a controlled background area
- Use an overlay
- Use an appropriate monochromatic version

Do not place the logo over visually chaotic areas simply because the logo technically remains visible.

---

# 7. Logo Color

The logo should use its approved color treatment.

Do not recolor the logo arbitrarily.

### Approved approaches

- Official full-color logo
- Approved light version
- Approved black monochromatic version
- Approved white monochromatic version

Do not introduce colors outside the approved KaizenHacks brand system.

---

# 8. Logo Placement

The logo should normally be placed with deliberate alignment to the surrounding layout.

Preferred alignments include:

- Grid-aligned left
- Grid-aligned right
- Centered when appropriate
- Consistent placement within repeated materials

Avoid placing the logo randomly simply to fill empty space.

The logo should feel intentionally positioned within the composition.

---

# 9. Logo on Promotional Materials

The logo should identify KaizenHacks without overpowering the primary content.

For event and campaign materials:

- The event name may be visually dominant.
- KaizenHacks should remain clearly identifiable.
- The logo should not compete unnecessarily with event-specific branding.
- The appropriate logo variant should be selected based on the composition.

When an event is a KaizenHacks initiative, the relationship between the event identity and KaizenHacks should remain clear.

---

# 10. Logo on Partner Materials

When KaizenHacks appears alongside partner or sponsor logos:

- Maintain clear space around the KaizenHacks logo.
- Keep logos visually balanced.
- Do not distort the KaizenHacks logo to match another logo's proportions.
- Do not allow partner logos to overlap the KaizenHacks clear-space area.
- Follow the partner's own logo requirements separately.

KaizenHacks should remain visually identifiable without attempting to dominate every partner logo.

---

# 11. Logo on Photography

When placing the logo over photography:

1. Check contrast.
2. Check the visual complexity behind the logo.
3. Select the appropriate light or dark version.
4. Maintain clear space.
5. Avoid placing the logo over important subjects.

The logo should identify the material without interfering with the photograph.

---

# 12. Logo Distortion

The logo must always maintain its original proportions.

### Never:

- Stretch horizontally
- Stretch vertically
- Compress the logo
- Skew the logo
- Rotate the logo
- Warp the logo
- Alter individual proportions
- Reconstruct the logo manually

Always scale the logo proportionally.

---

# 13. Logo Effects

Do not add visual effects to the logo unless the effect is explicitly approved as part of a specific campaign or application.

Avoid:

- Drop shadows
- Outer glows
- Bevels
- 3D effects
- Unapproved gradients
- Blurs
- Embossing
- Distortion effects
- Excessive opacity

The logo itself should remain clean and recognizable.

---

# 14. Do Not Modify the Logo

Never:

- Change the logo's typography
- Replace the logo typeface
- Rearrange logo components
- Remove important components
- Add symbols to the logo
- Add text to the logo
- Change proportions
- Redraw the mark
- Apply arbitrary colors
- Combine it with unrelated graphics as though they are part of the official logo

If a new logo configuration is needed, create and approve it as a new official logo asset.

---

# 15. Logo and Typography

The logo is an independent brand asset.

Do not recreate the logo using the KaizenHacks typography system.

The approved logo artwork should always be used instead of typing "KaizenHacks" and attempting to reproduce the logo manually.

---

# 16. Logo and Background Color

The logo should work naturally with the KaizenHacks color palette.

Preferred combinations include:

### Light

**Off White + standard logo**

### Dark

**Deep Navy + light logo**

### Editorial

**Warm Paper + dark logo**

### High Contrast

**Ink + white logo**

The logo should remain the strongest identifier regardless of the selected background.

---

# 17. Prototype Logos

The `Prototypes/` folder contains experimental or historical logo concepts.

Prototype logos are **not approved brand assets**.

They should not be used in:

- Official communication
- Public websites
- Presentations
- Social media
- Events
- Partnerships
- Marketing materials

unless the specific prototype has been formally approved and moved into the appropriate production logo category.

---

# 18. File Selection

Use the highest-quality available logo asset appropriate for the application.

Prefer:

- SVG for scalable digital applications
- High-resolution PNG when transparency is required
- Appropriate raster formats for applications that do not support SVG

Do not repeatedly convert compressed raster files and use them as new masters.

Editable/source artwork should be preserved separately in `Source_Files/`.

---

# 19. Logo Consistency

The logo should look the same regardless of where it appears.

Across:

- Website
- Social media
- Presentations
- Documents
- Posters
- Events
- Partnerships
- Digital products
- Printed materials

the same approved logo system should be used.

Consistency builds recognition and trust.

---

# 20. Quick Reference

| Situation | Preferred Logo |
|---|---|
| General brand use | Primary |
| Wide layout | Horizontal |
| Portrait/compact layout | Stacked |
| Small brand mark | Iconic |
| Typography-focused composition | Typographic |
| Website favicon | Favicon |
| Single-color application | Monochromatic |
| Dark background | Light version |
| Light background | Standard/dark version |
| Experimental concept | Prototype — not for production |

---

# 21. Final Rule

**Use the approved logo. Preserve its proportions. Give it space. Maintain contrast. Do not modify it.**

When in doubt, use the **Primary Logo** unless the layout clearly requires another approved variant.
