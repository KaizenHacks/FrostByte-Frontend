# KaizenHacks Brand Colors

## 1. Overview

The KaizenHacks color system is built around a deep blue foundation supported by lighter blue tones, warm paper neutrals, and strong dark/light neutrals.

The palette is designed to communicate:

- Trust
- Professionalism
- Intelligence
- Innovation
- Openness
- Creativity
- Longevity

The primary visual character comes from the relationship between **Deep Navy**, **Royal Blue**, and the **Warm Paper / Off White** neutrals.

---

# 3. Deep Navy

**HEX:** `#091F42`  
**RGB:** `9, 31, 66`

### Role

Deep Navy is the **primary KaizenHacks brand color**.

It establishes the core visual identity and should be the most recognizable color in the palette.

### Recommended Uses

- Primary backgrounds
- Brand headers
- Presentation covers
- Posters
- Major graphic compositions
- Large sections
- Primary brand surfaces
- Dark-mode foundations

### Character

Deep Navy communicates:

- Trust
- Stability
- Intelligence
- Professionalism
- Technical credibility

---

# 4. Royal Blue

**HEX:** `#254B85`  
**RGB:** `37, 75, 133`

### Role

Royal Blue is the main **secondary brand color**.

It provides stronger visual energy than Deep Navy while remaining professional.

### Recommended Uses

- Secondary backgrounds
- Cards
- Sections
- Supporting graphics
- Secondary emphasis
- Event materials
- Highlighted areas

### Character

Royal Blue communicates:

- Energy
- Innovation
- Confidence
- Progress

---

# 5. Mid Blue

**HEX:** `#3E70A5`  
**RGB:** `62, 112, 165`

### Role

Mid Blue is the primary **accent color**.

### Recommended Uses

- Links
- Icons
- Borders
- Small highlights
- Active states
- Data visualization
- Secondary emphasis

It should generally be used in smaller quantities than Deep Navy or Royal Blue.

---

# 6. Muted Blue

**HEX:** `#6499C1`  
**RGB:** `100, 153, 193`

### Role

Muted Blue is a **supporting and softer blue**.

### Recommended Uses

- Illustrations
- Decorative graphics
- Subtle backgrounds
- Gradients
- Charts
- Secondary visual elements
- Event-specific graphics

Muted Blue should not replace Deep Navy as the primary brand color.

---

# 7. Warm Paper

**HEX:** `#CAC4BA`  
**RGB:** `202, 196, 186`

### Role

Warm Paper is the palette's **cream/warm neutral**.

It introduces warmth into the otherwise blue-dominant system.

### Recommended Uses

- Editorial layouts
- Posters
- Printed materials
- Document backgrounds
- Cards
- Photography frames
- Editorial campaigns
- Selected social graphics

### Character

Warm Paper creates a more:

- Editorial
- Established
- Human
- Tactile
- Refined

visual character.

It should be treated as a supporting neutral rather than the primary brand color.

---

# 8. Off White

**HEX:** `#F2F3F1`  
**RGB:** `242, 243, 241`

### Role

Off White is the primary **light neutral**.

### Recommended Uses

- Light backgrounds
- Documents
- Presentations
- Website backgrounds
- Cards
- Social graphics
- Negative space

Off White should generally be preferred over pure white when the design benefits from a softer visual appearance.

---

# 9. Ink

**HEX:** `#0D1014`  
**RGB:** `13, 16, 20`

### Role

Ink is the primary **dark neutral**.

### Recommended Uses

- Body text
- Very dark backgrounds
- Footer areas
- Fine print
- High-contrast text
- Overlays

Ink should generally be used for text and deep neutral surfaces rather than replacing Deep Navy as the brand color.

---

# 10. Color Hierarchy

The palette follows this hierarchy:

### Primary

`#091F42` — Deep Navy

`#254B85` — Royal Blue

`#3E70A5` — Mid Blue

`#6499C1` — Muted Blue

### Warm Neutral

`#CAC4BA` — Warm Paper

`#F2F3F1` — Off White

`#0D1014` — Ink

# 12. Color Relationships

The palette is designed around contrast between:

**Deep / Dark**

`#091F42`  
`#0D1014`

and

**Light / Warm**

`#F2F3F1`  
`#CAC4BA`

with the blue range connecting the two.

This relationship should remain recognizable across different applications.
